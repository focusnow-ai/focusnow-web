FocusNow is built to be glanced at, not lived in. These shortcuts and habits keep it that way: in and out in seconds.

## The keyboard

On Windows, read **Ctrl** wherever you see **⌘**.

- **⌘K**: the command palette. Type where you want to go and press Enter. The fastest route to any page.
- **⌘\**: collapse the sidebar to icons. The same shortcut brings it back.

On the **Focus** page, when you're not typing in a field:

- **Space**: start or pause the timer.
- **1 – 4**: switch mode (Pomodoro, Deep Work, Short Break, Long Break).
- **T**: add a new task.

## The ⓘ buttons

Any time a metric makes you pause (*what exactly counts as "neutral"?*), look for the small **ⓘ** next to it. Every card and chart carries a plain-language explanation of what it measures and what's excluded, in your language. No metric in FocusNow should require faith.

## Small habits that pay off

- **Open the day, not the week.** "What was that at 3pm?" is fastest in **Calendar**: open the day and the records are listed by window title.
- **Search, don't scroll.** Activities and the Calendar day view both have search. Looking for one site or document title beats scanning a list.
- **Pause from the widget.** During a session, the floating widget has its own pause and resume button. No need to find the main window when the doorbell rings.
- **Fix categories the moment you notice.** Wrong category in Activities? ⋯ → Change category takes five seconds and makes every future day more accurate. Your [Focus Score](/guide/focus-score) is only as honest as your categories.
- **Log while it's fresh.** If you bill clients, spend two minutes each morning adding yesterday's records to [timecards](/guide/timecards). Friday-you will thank Tuesday-you.
