FocusNow is built to be glanced at, not lived in. These shortcuts and habits keep it that way — in and out in seconds.

## The keyboard

On Windows, read **Ctrl** wherever you see **⌘**.

- **⌘K** — the command palette. Type where you want to go, hit Enter. The fastest route to anything.
- **⌘1 – ⌘7** — jump straight to a view, in sidebar order: Dashboard, Analytics, Insights, Activities, Calendar, Sessions, Settings.
- **⌘\\** — collapse the sidebar to icons. Same shortcut brings it back.

## The ⓘ buttons

Any time a metric makes you pause — *what exactly counts as "neutral"?* — look for the small **ⓘ** next to it. Every card and chart carries a plain-language explanation of what it measures and what's excluded, in your language. No metric in FocusNow should require faith.

## Small habits that pay off

- **Click the timeline.** Any block on the Dashboard timeline opens a detail panel — what app, how long, what it counted as. The fastest way to answer "what was that red stretch at 3pm?"
- **Search, don't scroll.** Activities and the Calendar day view both have search. Looking for one specific site or document title beats scanning a list.
- **Pause from the widget.** During a session, the floating widget has its own pause/resume button — no need to find the main window when the doorbell rings.
- **Fix categories the moment you notice.** Wrong category on the timeline? ⋯ → Change category takes five seconds now and makes every future day more accurate. Your [Focus Score](/guide/focus-score) is only as honest as your categories.
