A timecard answers "what did I do?" A work statement answers "what am I asking this client to pay for?" The **Billing** page is where one becomes the other, and **Reports** shows how the month is adding up.

One line before we start: a work statement is a record of your work, **not a tax invoice**. FocusNow doesn't send invoices or collect payments. Create the invoice in your accounting tool or invoicing service, then note its number on the statement.

## Ready to bill

The top of Billing lists every client with billable time that isn't on a statement yet: how many entries, the date span, the hours and the amount, with a **Create statement** button beside each.

Projects without a client appear separately. They can't go on a statement until you assign a client on the Projects page.

## Prepare a work statement

*Create statement* opens a preview where you decide how it reads:

- **Line style**: *Detailed* lists every entry with its narrative; *Project summary* shows one line per project.
- **Rounding**: none, or round up to 5, 6, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The preview shows the tracked time next to the rounded time, so nothing is hidden from you.
- **Business details and footer note**: your business name and details at the top, and a note at the bottom for payment terms or thanks.

Every entry needs a price in a single currency. If a rate is missing, set it on the project or task first (the Timecards review flags tell you where).

## Finalize means locked

When the preview looks right, press **Finalize**. The statement's entries are locked and marked *billed*, so a billed entry can't change underneath the statement you sent. Then:

- **Download PDF** to send to your client, or export **CSV**.
- Fill in the **Invoice reference** with the number from your own invoice.

Need to fix something after finalizing? **Void** the statement. It stays in your records as void, and its entries go back to *not billed* so you can correct them and bill again.

## After you send it

Statements move from **Billed** to **Paid** when you press *Mark paid*; *Undo paid* reverses it. A paid statement can't be voided. Undo paid first if you really need to.

## Coverage: what's still missing

Further down, Billing compares what you tracked with what reached a timecard:

- **Tracked**, **On timecards**, **Coverage** and **Not on a timecard**, plus a day-by-day chart of tracked time against timecard time.
- A list of **tracked time not on a timecard yet**, grouped by window title or by app and site. Select what belongs to a client and press **Add to timecard**.

Not everything on that list is billable, and it isn't meant to be. It's simply where forgotten work shows up before the month closes.

## Reports and utilization

**Reports** summarizes your timecards for any range, with the same filters as Timecards:

- **Logged**, **Non-billable**, **Not billed**, **Billed**, **Paid** and **Amount**, kept per currency.
- **Utilization**: billable time as a share of your available working hours, shown against a 70-75% target band. It uses your [working hours](/guide/working-hours), so set those first.
- **Group by** project, client, task, tag, day or billable, then optionally by a second level. *Detailed* shows the entries behind each group, and **Export CSV** takes it all with you.

A good monthly rhythm: check Coverage, add what's missing, close the month's entries, create one statement per client, and glance at utilization before planning the next month.
