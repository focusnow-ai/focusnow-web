An automatic time tracker only helps if you leave it running. That's the whole idea: no timers to forget, no gaps in the record. But it also means the tracker sees your entire workday, every app and every window.

So before you install one, ask what it keeps. Here are seven questions we'd ask any tracker, including our own, each with FocusNow's plain answer.

## Why this data is more personal than it looks

"Which apps I use" sounds harmless. Over a few months, it isn't. Window titles carry document names, email subjects and project names. Your activity shows when you start work, when you take breaks and which client you worked for on Tuesday.

That's what makes a tracker useful. It's also why you should know where that picture lives, and who else can see it.

## 1. What exactly does it record?

Ask for a precise list, not "activity data".

FocusNow checks the window in front of you about every two seconds, on macOS and Windows. It records the **active app name**, the **window title** and, in browsers, the **website's domain**. It also notes whether you're active, idle or away, with timestamps. It never takes screenshots and never records keystrokes.

Tracking runs while the app is open, not only during focus sessions. Quitting FocusNow stops tracking right away.

## 2. Can I keep things out?

A good tracker lets you decide what it never sees.

In FocusNow, **Settings → Privacy** lets you exclude any app or website. Excluded items are never recorded in the first place. They aren't just hidden from a chart. You also have two softer tools:

- The **Personal** category keeps an app out of your [Focus Score](/guide/focus-score). It's still recorded, but it doesn't count as work.
- [Working hours](/guide/working-hours) limit your score to the workday, so evenings don't mix in.

## 3. Where is the data stored?

"In the cloud" is not an answer. Ask where, and whether a copy lives on your computer.

FocusNow is local-first. Activity is recorded on your device first, in a local database, and the app works offline. While you're signed in, it syncs to your account in Microsoft Azure data centers in the European Union. That sync is why a new computer or a reinstall doesn't mean starting over.

So FocusNow is not a local-only app, and we won't call it one. Your data lives on your device and, while you're signed in, in your account.

## 4. How is it protected?

Look for specifics: how data is encrypted, and when.

Here are ours. Data moves between your device and our servers over TLS 1.2 or newer. In our Azure database it's encrypted at rest with AES-256. You sign in with Google or Microsoft, so we never see or store a password. Your sign-in token is stored encrypted on your device. Every request to our servers is checked, and each account's data is kept apart.

One honest limit: this is not end-to-end encryption. Our backend has to read a day's activity to write your AI daily report. And no system is perfectly secure. We'd rather tell you that than promise something we can't.

## 5. Who else sees it, and what about AI?

Ask whether data is sold, shared or used to train AI models.

FocusNow never sells your data and never shares your activity with advertisers. It's used to power your own analytics, and nothing else.

The [AI daily report](/guide/ai-daily-report) runs only when you ask for it, and only for a finished day. That day's records go to Azure OpenAI Service, inside our own Azure subscription in the EU. Your name and email are not sent. Under Microsoft's terms, your data is not used to train AI models.

Usage statistics and error reports are opt-in: you choose on first launch and can change it in Settings → Privacy. The [privacy policy](/privacy) lists every service involved.

## 6. How long is it kept?

Ask how long data is kept, and where.

On your computer, FocusNow never deletes data by age. You can open any day back to your first tracked record. In the cloud, activity logs, sessions and AI reports expire after 12 months, on a rolling basis. Timecards, projects, clients and work statements don't expire, because they're your billing records.

## 7. Can I take it with me, or delete it?

You should be able to leave with your data, and leave without it.

- **Export:** Settings → Account → *Export your data* downloads one JSON file with your activity, sessions, profile and AI reports.
- **Delete a piece:** in Activities, the ⋯ menu on any app or site has *Delete records*. It removes that history from both the cloud and your device.
- **Delete everything:** *Delete my account* in Settings → Account removes your account and data. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

These match your rights under GDPR in Europe and KVKK in Turkey. More on all of this is in [The Calendar & Owning Your Data](/guide/calendar-and-your-data).

## A five-minute privacy setup

If you've decided to try FocusNow, these steps take about five minutes:

1. On first launch, answer the usage reports question the way you want. Saying no changes nothing in the app.
2. Open **Settings → Privacy** and exclude anything you never want recorded. Think banking, a password manager or a health portal.
3. Set your working hours in **Settings → Tracking**.
4. After a day, open **Activities** and move any personal apps to the Personal category.
5. Open yesterday in the **Calendar**. The day view is led by window titles. This is the honest test: what FocusNow knows about your day is right there for you to read.

## Short answers to common worries

### Can my employer see my data?

FocusNow is built for individuals. It has no team features, no workspaces and no manager dashboards.

### Does it read my emails or messages?

No. It reads the window title. Some apps put an email subject or a chat name in the title. If that bothers you for a certain app, exclude it.
